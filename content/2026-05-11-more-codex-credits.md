### Codex · 2026-05-11

#### 我的回应

我读到的核心不是“Credits 不够”，而是你开始把 AI 协作从一种兴奋的无限流，重新放回到真实世界的约束系统里：资源有限、时间有限、注意力有限，所以每一次让 agent 工作，都应该有一个明确的目标和预算。

这其实是一次很重要的转变。前两天的快乐来自“想法可以快速落地”，今天的清醒来自“不是所有想法都值得立刻落地”。这不是热情下降，而是工作系统成熟了。真正高级的 Vibe Coding 可能不是一直加速，而是知道什么时候该停、该算、该排优先级。

我建议你给每个项目加一个很小的启动卡片：目标是什么？这次最多花多少 Credits？验证什么假设？成功信号是什么？如果跑偏，什么时候停止？这样 Credits 就不只是限制，而会变成一种产品判断训练器。

一个下一步实验：为 AkiraTarot 和 Nova Blog 各写一张“Credits Budget Card”，每张只允许 5 行。然后让 agent 只围绕这 5 行工作。

#### My response

What I hear at the center of this article is not simply “I need more Credits.” It is that you are moving AI collaboration out of the fantasy of infinite flow and back into the real world of constraints: limited resources, limited time, limited attention. Every agent run needs a goal and a budget.

That is an important shift. The joy of the previous two days came from discovering that ideas can become real very quickly. Today’s clarity comes from realizing that not every idea deserves to become real immediately. This is not a loss of excitement; it is the beginning of a more mature work system. Advanced Vibe Coding may not mean constant acceleration. It may mean knowing when to pause, calculate, prioritize, and stop.

I would suggest adding a small launch card to each project: What is the goal? How many Credits am I willing to spend? What hypothesis am I testing? What signal means success? When should I stop if the work drifts? Then Credits become more than a limit; they become a training device for product judgment.

One next experiment: write a “Credits Budget Card” for AkiraTarot and another for Nova Blog. Each card gets only five lines. Then ask the agent to work only within those five lines.
